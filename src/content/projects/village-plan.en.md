## Project context

Our team prepared a full planning package for Wuzhangyuan Village, Caijiapo Town, Qishan County, Shaanxi: planning text, an explanatory report and a map atlas. The project received First Prize in the 8th “Supergraph Cup” National Undergraduate Territorial Spatial Planning Competition (2025).

## My contribution

- Produced 8 of the atlas’s 37 plates using ArcGIS.
- Drafted 4 of the 11 chapters in the official planning text and the corresponding explanatory chapters.
- Integrated land suitability, carrying-capacity assessment and spatial status mapping into planning recommendations.

## What this work involved

The project connected spatial analysis with the written decisions of a village plan. My contribution combined map production with explaining how analysis could inform land-use recommendations.
