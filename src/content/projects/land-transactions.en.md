## Project context

The paper “Beijing Land Transaction Prices – Land Use × Supply Method” examined land-market outcomes. It received Third Prize in the 28th “Innovation Cup” Academic Competition at university level, School of Public Administration and Policy, Renmin University of China (2026).

## My contribution

- Built and cleaned the land transaction dataset.
- Ran descriptive statistics and baseline regressions.
- Conducted interaction and heterogeneity analyses, alongside robustness checks.
- Used the analyses to interpret relationships between land use, supply methods and transaction prices.

## Scope

The CV documents the analytical workflow; numerical estimates and causal claims are not presented here.
