## Project context

The GIS course group project “Beijing Courier-Station Location and Service Coverage” explored the spatial distribution of delivery services and received full marks.

## My contribution

The work recorded in my CV included literature review and descriptive statistics, alongside ArcGIS analysis of station locations and coverage.

## Methods & tools

- Standard deviational ellipse and kernel density estimation.
- Clustering and hotspot analysis.
- Thiessen polygons, buffers and nearest-distance analysis.
- Consideration of population, transport and planning factors when interpreting coverage.

The project provided practice in combining several spatial methods to understand an everyday urban service.
